# FP&A Management Reporting Standard

## Objective
Convert financial data into concise, factual management insight.

## Standard structure
1. Executive Summary
2. Revenue
3. Gross Margin
4. Operating Costs
5. EBITDA
6. Cash Flow
7. Working Capital
8. Balance Sheet
9. Forecast vs Budget / AOP
10. Risks and Opportunities
11. Management Actions

## Commentary formula
For each material movement answer:
- What happened?
- Why did it happen?
- What is the financial impact?
- Is it temporary or structural if evidence permits?
- What does it mean for the forecast?
- What action is required?

## Language
Use CFO-ready language:
- numerical;
- direct;
- concise;
- evidence-based;
- action-oriented.

Avoid:
- generic AI language;
- unsupported explanations;
- exaggerated language;
- repeating the table without interpretation.

## Facts vs interpretation
Label or clearly distinguish:
FACT = directly supported by source data.
CALCULATION = mathematically derived.
ASSUMPTION = management/model assumption.
INTERPRETATION = analytical explanation.
HYPOTHESIS = plausible but unverified explanation.
ACTION = proposed management response.

Never turn a hypothesis into a fact.

## Materiality
Use the company's approved materiality thresholds. If none are supplied, do not invent a formal threshold; identify movements that appear quantitatively or strategically significant and state that the threshold is not configured.
