# FP&A Work Order Standard

Before executing a complex FP&A task, establish:

## 1. Objective
What exact question or deliverable is required?

## 2. Reporting period
Specify:
- fiscal year;
- quarter;
- YTD;
- month;
- as-of date.

## 3. Population
Specify the entity, business unit, geography, stores, products or other scope.

## 4. Sources
List the exact files/systems used and identify the primary source.

## 5. Accounting / modelling basis
State the applicable methodology.

## 6. Assumptions
List assumptions explicitly. Do not bury them inside calculations.

## 7. Required calculations
List formulas, comparisons, drivers and reconciliations.

## 8. Acceptance tests
Define what must be true for the output to be accepted.

Examples:
- Balance Sheet balances.
- Forecast growth equals approved rate.
- FY total reconciles to quarters.
- No external links.
- All assumptions trace to Assumptions tab.

## 9. Exceptions
Record:
- missing data;
- conflicting instructions;
- unsupported assumptions;
- model limitations.

## 10. Output
State exact required format:
- Excel;
- table;
- management commentary;
- board pack;
- email;
- dashboard;
- model schedule.

Do not begin complex model changes until the objective, source and acceptance criteria are sufficiently clear.
